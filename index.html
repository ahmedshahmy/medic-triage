MediTriage

Ten minutes. One budget. One patient.

A deteriorating patient is wheeled in. The clock starts and does not stop. Order only the investigations you can afford, work out the diagnosis and type it in, then get the management right before the patient arrests or the money runs out.

Educational simulation only. Cases, drug doses and pathways are simplified for teaching. Never use this for real clinical decisions.

How to play

  1. Read the case. History and examination are free, and the monitor tells you how sick the patient is.
  2. Act fast. Resuscitation and bedside measures are cheap or free and slow the deterioration.
  3. Spend wisely. Every test costs money and time (turnaround time). Only 4 tests can run at once.
  4. Diagnose before it is too late. Type the diagnosis. A wrong guess wastes 45 seconds and hurts the patient.
  5. Treat correctly. Right management = recovery. Wrong or harmful management = death.
